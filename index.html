Printers Maintenance

regular cleaning of the exterior and internal components with a dry, lint-free cloth or compressed air to remove dust and debris, typically on a monthly basis.

  • Cleaning Routine: Clean the printer exterior with a soft cloth and use compressed air for the interior to remove debris that causes paper jams.
  • Software Updates: Regularly check the manufacturer's website (e.g., HP) for firmware updates to fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Safety Precautions:When removing jams, do not force the paper, as this can damage the internal components.
